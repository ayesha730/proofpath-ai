ProofPath AI — Project Scope

Overview

ProofPath AI helps students discover scholarships, fellowships, hackathons, research opportunities, exchange programs, internships, and other career-building opportunities.

Problem

Students often struggle to find relevant opportunities because information is scattered across different websites. They may also spend time reviewing opportunities for which they are not eligible.

Target Users

- Undergraduate and graduate students
- Students seeking fully funded international education
- Students looking for hackathons, fellowships, research, and internships

Core Features

- Search for opportunities across supported sources.
- Filter opportunities by category and user preferences.
- Match opportunities to a student's profile.
- Prioritize funding preferences and relevant eligibility criteria.
- Display opportunity details and application links.

Project Boundaries

ProofPath AI is an opportunity-discovery and matching assistant. It does not guarantee eligibility, funding, or selection. Users should verify deadlines and requirements on the official opportunity website.

Intended Outcome

Reduce the time students spend searching for opportunities and help them identify relevant options more efficiently.
